Privacy Policy

App: AuraVault Chess (com.auravaultchess) • Last Updated: November 2026

1. Information We Collect

AuraVault Chess minimizes data collection so you can focus on the board. Depending on how you use the app, the following information may be processed:

  • Guest Account Mode: Generates a local session identifier on your device. Ratings, match history, and preferences remain stored locally on your device.
  • Google Sign-In / Email Account (Optional): Uses Firebase Authentication and Google Credential Manager to verify your account, receiving your basic user ID, display name, and email address (your email is never shown publicly).
  • Profile & Public Leaderboard Projection: For authenticated accounts, your chosen avatar symbol, display name, title badge, rating, win/loss/draw totals, win streak, and favored opening may sync to Firebase Firestore under your user ID with zero private contact details exposed.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • Operate core gameplay features, Glicko-1 ratings, Daily Tasks, and PGN match archives.
  • Sync profile preferences and public leaderboard standings across authenticated sessions.
  • Automated Opponent Fallback: When no real player match is available, pair you with a skill-calibrated in-game AI opponent without sharing personal identity information.

3. Storage, Caching & Security

Local game records reside in private Android app storage. Cloud-synced attributes are protected via Firebase Authentication and Firestore security rules. Global leaderboard entries are cached locally on your device for 1 hour and win uploads are throttled to at most once per hour to protect battery life and minimize network usage. We never sell personal data.

4. Advertising & Diagnostics (If Enabled)

Future versions of the app may display optional Rewarded or Interstitial ads via third-party ad networks (such as Google AdMob) and collect anonymous crash diagnostics (such as Firebase Crashlytics). Where required by law (e.g., EEA/UK), a privacy consent prompt will be presented before serving personalized ads.

5. Account Deletion & Data Control

You may play in Guest Mode without creating an account, or sign out and permanently delete your cloud account, profile, and leaderboard entry at any time inside the app via Profile → Delete Account.

If you no longer have access to the device and want your cloud data deleted, email us at [YOUR_SUPPORT_EMAIL] with your account email.
