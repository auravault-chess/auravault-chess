Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026 • App: AuraVault Chess

1. Information We Collect

AuraVault Chess minimizes data collection so you can focus on the board. Depending on how you use the app, we process:

  • Guest Account Mode: Generates a local session identifier on your device. Your ratings, match archives, and preferences remain stored locally on your device.
  • Google Sign-In / Email Account (Optional): Uses Firebase Authentication to verify your account, receiving your user ID, display name, and email address (never shared publicly).
  • Profile & Public Leaderboard Data: Authenticated players' display name, title badge, chosen avatar symbol, rating, win/loss/draw totals, win streak, and favored opening may be stored in Cloud Firestore. Email addresses and private contact details are never exposed on the public leaderboard.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • Operate core chess gameplay, Glicko-1 ratings, Daily Tasks, and progression records.
  • Preserve PGN match history for your personal review and export.
  • Sync your avatar and public leaderboard standings across authenticated sessions.
  • Automated AI Fallback: When no live human opponent is queued, pair you with a skill-calibrated in-game AI bot without sharing personal identity data.

3. Caching, Crash Diagnostics & Security

Local match records stay inside private Android app storage. Cloud-synced profile and leaderboard data is protected by Firebase Authentication and Firestore Security Rules.

To reduce mobile network and battery usage, global leaderboard standings are cached locally on your device for 1 hour and rated win leaderboard uploads are throttled to at most once every 1 hour.

Firebase Crashlytics: We use Google Firebase Crashlytics to collect anonymous crash logs, stack traces, and basic device state diagnostics (with no personal contact info) strictly to fix bugs and improve app stability.

We do not sell your personal data or share personal data with third-party data brokers.

4. Third-Party Services

AuraVault Chess uses the following Google/Firebase infrastructure services:

  • Google Firebase Authentication (optional account login)
  • Google Cloud Firestore (authenticated profile & leaderboard storage)
  • Google Firebase Crashlytics (anonymous crash diagnostics)

5. Your Rights & Account / Data Deletion

You can play anytime in Guest Mode without creating an account.

In-App Deletion: Authenticated users can permanently delete their account, profile record, and public leaderboard entry immediately inside the app via Profile → Delete Account & Data.

Email Deletion Request: If you no longer have access to the device or need manual assistance deleting your cloud records, email auravaultchess@gmail.com from your registered email address with the subject "Account Deletion Request - AuraVault Chess", and your associated Firebase Auth and Firestore records will be removed.

6. Children's Privacy

AuraVault Chess does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the minimum digital consent age in your jurisdiction) without verifiable parental consent. If you believe a child has provided personal data, please contact us for immediate deletion.

7. Privacy Contact

For privacy inquiries, data access requests, or deletion assistance, contact the AuraVault Chess developer at: auravaultchess@gmail.com
